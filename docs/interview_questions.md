# Interview questions (V3, share with Lithasha)

Before you start: tell the person who you are, that this is for a university project, and ask permission to use a short quote (name not required).

1. Tell me about a normal day at your stall/shop. Which part takes the most time?
2. How do you keep track of what you sell and what you spend today? (memory, paper, phone, spreadsheet)
3. Do you sell on credit? How often, and who to? How do you remember who owes you?
4. Think of the last time someone owed you money. What happened? How did it feel to ask for it back?
5. How much money would you say goes unpaid in a month? What did you do about it?
6. Would you speak into your phone to record a sale ("Maya owes 1500 for cakes")? When would you not want to? (noise, privacy, language)
7. If an app sent your customer a polite SMS reminder for you, how would you feel? What would worry you?
8. Have you ever tried to get a loan or open a bank account? Was it hard to prove your income?

Notes to capture for each person: type of business, languages used, phone type, best quote.
